  1. 01

    Ask

    Plain conversation in Hebrew or English. Short answers by default, long ones when you ask for them.

    In build
  2. 02

    Translate

    Hebrew to English and back. Keeps tone, idiom and register instead of translating word for word.

    In build
  3. 03

    Sources

    Answers come with links you can open, so you can check a claim instead of taking our word for it.

    Planned
  4. 04

    One page each morning: what happened, why it matters, and where to read more.

    Planned
  5. 05

    Timeline

    Move through events by date, with maps and primary documents next to each entry.

    Planned
  6. 06
